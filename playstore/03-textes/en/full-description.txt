DungeonStep - March of Souls

Your real steps are the only resource in the game.

Every step you take during the day becomes the distance travelled by Kaelen, a pilgrim descending the dungeons to free the souls held there. Ten steps make one metre. One hundred metres make one floor. And every floor is sealed by a guardian.

You do not control Kaelen: he walks, fights and falls on his own. You choose what matters - the gear to keep, the upgrades to pay for, when to rest at camp, which step source to connect.

WHAT AWAITS YOU
 8 pixel-art biomes, from the Crypts of Vael to the Throne of Souls.
 A guardian on every floor, with its attacks and weaknesses.
 Automatic combat, fervour that rises with your steps, chests to force open.
 Gear to compare, sets to complete, items to fuse.
 A camp to rest, gear up and set out again.
 A Codex with permanent collections, the monsters, guardians and sets you meet.
 A journal and a legend that tell the walk.

YOUR STEPS, YOUR PACE
 10 steps = 1 metre, 1,000 steps = 1 floor.
 Sources: Garmin (Personal API) or Health Connect, one at a time.
 First connection: only the day's steps are credited. After that, the game catches up to 7 days, never before the first-connection date.
 Cap of 60,000 steps per day, 100,000 steps per sync, 250 steps/minute beyond which steps are ignored.
 No GPS, no game account: progress stays on your phone.

THE PROMISE
No ads, no in-app purchases, no leaderboard. Just your steps, a pilgrim, and souls waiting.

DungeonStep is a personal walking game. It needs a step source: without Garmin or Health Connect, Kaelen stands still.
