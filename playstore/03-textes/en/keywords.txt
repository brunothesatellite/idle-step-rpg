# Keywords (ASO reference, not a Play Console field)

The title and the short description are the only fields indexed by Play search. These words guide the copy and the campaigns, without keyword stuffing.

- walking game
- step counter RPG
- idle RPG
- step role-playing game
- pixel dungeon
- dark fantasy
- Garmin
- Health Connect
- game without ads
- auto-battler RPG

Avoid: "best RPG", "#1", "millions of players", "free" if a paid step source is required, and any promise of money or real-world rewards.
