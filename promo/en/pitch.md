# Pitch — The March of Souls

## Reddit title

**Android walking RPG where your steps are the only resource (free, no ads, no purchases, no AI, open source)**

## Main text (Reddit / Facebook)

Hi,

I'm someone who walks (a bit) and mostly runs (a lot). A former developer, I keep tinkering with apps and games in my spare time. For a while I'd been looking for something that mixes running / walking with a game (something like Stepcraft for those in the know, but less of a contraption). A role-playing game based on the steps I take every day. I ended up coding **DungeonStep** (The March of Souls): a pixel-art RPG that is a little (a lot) old-school, where you follow a hero (Kaelen) fighting through an endless dungeon. Except his only resource is **your steps**!

The principle is simple: **no steps, no progress**. No ads, no purchases, no Instagram sharing or whatever to earn steps. The only way is your real steps, in your real life, even when the weather is awful. Kaelen moves when you walk, runs when you run, and stops when you stop. Steps fill a reserve; that reserve is used to walk through the dungeon, start fights, open chests and farm. When the reserve is empty, Kaelen eventually stops.

It is partly an "idle" game in the sense that you don't need to stare at it: you go for your walk, come back, and Kaelen has moved on (he spends a fraction of the steps you took to advance and fight on his own), then you equip gear, upgrade your character and set off again. But it is not a game that runs by itself: without your steps, nothing happens. And sometimes it is worth spending time improving your gear to progress faster.

What I wanted, and what I want to keep:

- **No advertising.** (though Franck Leboeuf trying to sell your car would have made me laugh in the game)
- **No in-app purchases.** Everything is free, and everything will stay free. I'm an (almost) old fool who saw the dawn of the internet and still believes in sharing without payment.
- **No AI-generated content.** The texts are written by hand; I do write part of my code and docs with AI help (VS Code / GitHub Copilot / Opencode) — like every developer in 2026, right? The visuals are pixel art, not generated images.
- **No data collection.** The app has no server of its own, no account with me, no analytics. Steps are read locally on your phone (Health Connect), or through your Garmin source (your own Garmin API key, which you provide to the app) if you choose that option. Nothing is resold.
- **The code is fully available** on GitHub. You can read it, build it, modify it, audit it (and fix my bugs!), fork it.

At first it was a personal project for me and a few runner friends. It has grown a lot, and I'm happy to share it more widely. I make nothing from it (I'm not about to repay the €25 needed to open the Google Play Store account); if it entertains you, that's already a lot.

Two ways to install it:

- **Play Store**: https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.brunothesatellite.idlesteprpg
- **GitHub** (APK to install manually, sideloading; Android will show a warning, that's normal): https://github.com/brunothesatellite/idle-step-rpg/releases/latest

The GitHub version is updated **more often** than the Play Store one (app review on the Play Store takes longer, so I publish there much less regularly than on GitHub).

The project and the code: https://github.com/brunothesatellite/idle-step-rpg

A presentation video: https://www.youtube.com/shorts/E51AA6Y6Rg8

The app requires **Android 14 or later**. If you try it, tell me what you like or what blocks you. I'm especially curious whether the "I walk → my character moves" rhythm holds up over time.

Happy walking, happy running!
